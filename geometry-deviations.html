As-built departure

A structural element is outside its design position or tolerance

We incorporate the measured geometry into the analysis and assess eccentricity, load transfer, stability, connections and construction interfaces.

Typical cases

  • column or wall offset, lean or reduced section;
  • slab level, thickness or support deviation;
  • misplaced embedded plates, anchors or openings;
  • foundation or pile head position outside tolerance;
  • mismatch between structural and façade or MEP interfaces.

Possible responses

Acceptance with documented calculation, interface modification, local load redistribution, connection redesign, repair or strengthening.

Send for review

Survey report, design coordinates and levels, element drawings, photographs and the next construction operation.
