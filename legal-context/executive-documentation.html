Traceability

Close-out records must show what was found, decided and built

A technical solution is complete only when the approved response, inspection evidence and final as-built condition can be traced by future project teams.

Recommended record

  • registered deviation or defect report;
  • survey, photographs and test evidence;
  • calculation note and approved decision;
  • repair or strengthening drawings;
  • inspection and material records;
  • final as-built confirmation and monitoring requirements.
