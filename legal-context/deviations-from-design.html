Decision framework

Managing a departure from design

Record the actual condition, identify the affected requirements, quantify structural impact, agree the approval route and issue a controlled technical response before affected work continues.

Minimum engineering record

  • location and unambiguous description;
  • survey, photographs and test evidence;
  • comparison with the approved design;
  • calculation and decision criteria;
  • approved repair, acceptance or strengthening solution;
  • verification and close-out evidence.
