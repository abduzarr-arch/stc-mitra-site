Risk control

Pause only the work affected by unresolved structural risk

When evidence is incomplete or a deviation may affect stability, strength or the safety of subsequent operations, the affected zone should be controlled until an authorised decision is issued.

Immediate actions

  • define and secure the affected area;
  • prevent additional loading or irreversible work;
  • record the actual condition before alteration;
  • identify temporary support or monitoring needs;
  • set clear technical criteria for release of work.
