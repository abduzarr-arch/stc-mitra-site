Scope definition

Commission the decision, not only the calculation

A useful brief states the decision required, governing code, available evidence, affected programme, approval route and expected deliverable.

Include in the brief

  • the engineering question and required decision date;
  • project stage and parties who will rely on the output;
  • governing ACI, EN/Eurocode, SP or local requirements;
  • drawings, models, surveys, tests and known limitations;
  • format of calculation, report, drawings and site support;
  • review and approval responsibilities.
