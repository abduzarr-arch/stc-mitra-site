Construction defect

Reinforcement has been cut, displaced or damaged

We determine the structural role of the affected bars, quantify the lost capacity and design a practical reinstatement or strengthening solution.

Engineering questions

  • which force path and limit state the bars support;
  • whether anchorage, lap length or confinement is compromised;
  • how the damage interacts with cracks, openings and construction joints;
  • whether temporary unloading or propping is required;
  • what repair sequence is feasible on site.

Possible responses

Acceptance with justification, replacement bars with verified anchorage, mechanical couplers, bonded reinforcement, steel elements or a local reinforced-concrete strengthening detail.

Send for review

Element drawings, bar schedule, photos with scale, damage location, concrete strength and current loads.
