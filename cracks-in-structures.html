Observed structural behaviour

Cracks have appeared in slabs, walls, beams or foundations

We distinguish structural, restraint, thermal, shrinkage and settlement-related causes, then connect observed crack patterns to a verified structural model.

What matters

  • location, direction, width, depth and development over time;
  • loads and construction operations before cracking;
  • restraint, joints, temperature and shrinkage effects;
  • foundation movement and interaction with adjacent elements;
  • strength, serviceability, durability and water-tightness.

Possible responses

Monitoring, sealing or injection, load management, local repair, movement accommodation or strengthening based on the verified cause rather than the appearance alone.

Send for review

Marked-up plans, dated photos with scale, crack monitoring data, construction sequence and relevant material tests.
