Material non-conformance

Concrete strength is below the specified class

We reconcile test evidence, actual loading and structural reserve before recommending acceptance, further investigation, load restrictions or strengthening.

Assessment sequence

  • review sampling, curing and test reliability;
  • map the affected pours and structural elements;
  • define characteristic in-situ strength from credible evidence;
  • recalculate critical limit states using actual properties;
  • check construction sequence and early-age loading.

Possible outcomes

Additional testing, acceptance with calculation, revised loading sequence, local repair, jacketing, enlargement or other strengthening supported by verified analysis.

Send for review

Test reports, pour records, element drawings, concrete specification, loading history and construction programme.
