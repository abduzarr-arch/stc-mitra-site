Construction deviation

An opening was cut in a slab without a verified design

We check punching, bending, shear, deflection, load paths and reinforcement continuity, then define whether the opening can remain or requires local strengthening.

What the assessment covers

  • actual opening size, position and edge condition;
  • existing reinforcement and any bars already cut;
  • column, wall and concentrated-load proximity;
  • construction stage and loads already applied;
  • local and global structural response.

Possible outcomes

Acceptance as built, trimming reinforcement, steel framing, bonded or anchored reinforcement, local slab thickening, or a project-specific strengthening detail.

Send for review

Plan and reinforcement drawings, opening dimensions, photographs, scan results where available, and current loading status.
