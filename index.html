Specialist calculation work

Where simplified checks stop being sufficient

We select the model complexity from the governing failure mechanism, not from software availability.

01

Nonlinear structural analysis

For redistribution, cracking, yielding, second-order effects and post-elastic behaviour.

Model
Material + geometric nonlinearity; contact and staged activation where governing.
Control
Load path, stiffness degradation, convergence, sensitivity and failure mechanism.
Output
Capacity, governing zones and the minimum justified strengthening scope.
02

Connections and local FE models

For nodes whose behaviour cannot be represented by nominal member checks alone.

Model
Global actions transferred into local models of plates, welds, bolts, anchors and concrete zones.
Control
Contact, bearing, prying, local plasticity and force redistribution.
Output
Verified force path, component checks and buildable connection or strengthening details.
03

Thermal stress states

For restrained temperature deformation, gradients and construction-stage effects.

Model
Temperature fields, restraint conditions, stiffness evolution and erection or casting sequence.
Control
Imposed forces, cracking risk, deformation compatibility and sensitive restraints.
Output
Requirements for joints, reinforcement, release details or construction sequence.
