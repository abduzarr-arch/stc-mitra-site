Special analyses

Analysis scope selected from the governing structural response

A nonlinear or local model is introduced where the assumptions of a linear member check are insufficient. The report records model boundaries, load transfer, material laws and acceptance criteria.

01

Nonlinear structural analysis

Used where cracking, yielding, second-order response or contact changes the distribution of internal forces.

Model
Material laws for concrete, reinforcement and structural steel; geometric nonlinearity, contact and staged activation where required.
Control
Load-displacement response, stiffness degradation, convergence, boundary-condition sensitivity and the governing failure mechanism.
Output
Calculated capacity, governing zones and a justified strengthening scope.
02

Connections and local FE models

Applied after actions from the global model have been reduced to boundary conditions for the connection submodel.

Model
Plates, welds, bolts, anchors and concrete bearing zones with compatible restraints and load introduction.
Control
Contact, bearing, prying action, local plastic strain and redistribution between connection components.
Output
Documented force path, code checks of components and connection or strengthening details.
03

Thermal stress states

Used for restrained thermal deformation and construction sequences that generate imposed actions.

Model
Temperature fields or gradients, restraint conditions, stiffness at the relevant age and the erection or casting sequence.
Control
Imposed forces, cracking risk, deformation compatibility and sensitivity to support stiffness.
Output
Requirements for joints, reinforcement, release details and construction sequence.
